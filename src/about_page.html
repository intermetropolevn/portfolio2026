Profile Summary

A product builder with a growth engineer's brain.

I’m a Product Specialist with 9+ years of experience building and scaling solutions across SaaS, Web & Mobile, AI, Omnichannel, Web3, and complex E-commerce ecosystems.

My career began across the digital marketing spectrum—spanning strategy, SEO, social media, communications, and paid media—which shaped a strong foundation in user behavior, growth, and distribution. Building on that, I now work at the intersection of product, architecture, go-to-market, and operations—allowing me to approach product not as a standalone system, but as an integrated business engine.

I’m well-versed in AI transformation, UX, and design principles, and comfortable navigating both strategy and execution.

I’ve led 0→1 product development across multiple startups, shipped 20+ products into international markets, and contributed to multi-million dollar revenue growth. I've established and grown a Product org from scratch to <10 people, overseeing Engineer, UI/UX Designer, QC, Data and Research.

I’m driven by improving how products work—streamlining processes, elevating user experience, and leveraging AI to build smarter, more adaptive systems.
